CloakShot Privacy Policy

1. Core Privacy Commitment (100% Client-Side Execution)

CloakShot is engineered as a 100% browser-local privacy and demo studio for webpages. All DOM blurring, solid redaction, Smart Mock Swap generation, regex pattern scanning, Live Screen-Share Shielding, and scrolling screenshot stitching run strictly client-side inside your local Chrome browser.

Zero webpage data ever leaves your device. CloakShot does not operate any backend servers, does not use analytics or telemetry scripts, and never transmits, logs, stores remotely, or sells your webpage contents, screenshots, clipboard contents, or browsing history.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

CloakShot uses Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API solely to persist your local preferences and optional settings on your own machine:

  • User Preferences: Studio toggles such as Auto-Restore after Capture and Clean Frame backdrop preference.
  • Saved Domain Rules (Optional): Hostnames and Auto-Scan categories you explicitly choose to save so CloakShot can automatically mask sensitive fields when you revisit those domains. You can delete any saved domain rule at any time from the CloakShot popup.

Original webpage text and DOM snapshots used for instant Undo and Restore are held solely in volatile in-memory structures (WeakMap) for the current tab session and are discarded immediately when you restore, reload, or close the tab.

3. Payments & Pro License Verification (ExtensionPay & Stripe)

CloakShot offers an optional one-time Pro Lifetime Pass. Checkout, billing, and license verification are handled securely by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

When you purchase or restore a Pro license, ExtensionPay and Stripe process your payment details and email address strictly to complete your transaction and verify your Pro entitlement across Chrome installations. CloakShot itself never sees or stores your credit card details. For more details, please review ExtensionPay's Privacy Policy and Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. Chrome Permissions Transparency

CloakShot requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function locally:

  • activeTab & scripting: Injects the isolated Shadow DOM toolbar and masking engine into the current tab only when you trigger CloakShot via Alt+Shift+C (Option+Shift+C on macOS) or click the extension popup.
  • tabs: Identifies the active tab and captures the visible viewport via chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab when you initiate a screenshot.
  • clipboardWrite: Copies your locally rendered PNG screenshot directly to your system clipboard when you choose 1-click Copy PNG.
  • storage: Saves your local preferences and optional Saved Domain Rules in chrome.storage.local.
  • optional_host_permissions (<all_urls>): Not granted at install time. Requested dynamically for a specific hostname only if you explicitly click Save rules for [domain] so CloakShot can auto-apply your privacy shield on revisit.
